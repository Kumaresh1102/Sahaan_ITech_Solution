FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a typical project take?

A corporate website usually takes 2–4 weeks. Kiosk systems and custom platforms range from 6–16 weeks depending on scope. We give you a clear timeline after the free consultation.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a warranty period, and we offer ongoing maintenance plans with monitoring, updates and priority support.

Can you integrate with my existing POS or systems?

Absolutely. POS and system integration is one of our core specialities — our kiosks and platforms connect to common POS, payment and accounting systems.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes — from single-outlet restaurants and temples to enterprise groups. We scale the solution and pricing to fit your stage.

How do we get started?

Send us a message through the contact form or WhatsApp. We'll arrange a free consultation, understand your requirements, and send a detailed proposal and quote.
